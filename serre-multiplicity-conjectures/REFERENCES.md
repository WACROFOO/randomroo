# References

## Primary sources

1. J.-P. Serre, *Algèbre locale · Multiplicités*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics
   11, Springer, 1965 (from 1957/58 Collège de France lectures). English
   translation: *Local Algebra*, Springer Monographs in Mathematics, 2000.
   — Definition of $\chi$, reduction to the diagonal, proofs of M1 (general)
   and M1–M4 (equicharacteristic and unramified).

2. M. Auslander, D. Buchsbaum, *Codimension and multiplicity*, Ann. of Math.
   **68** (1958), 625–657. — Koszul Euler characteristic = Samuel multiplicity.

3. P. Roberts, *The vanishing of intersection multiplicities of perfect
   complexes*, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. **13** (1985), 127–130. — Vanishing (M3)
   via local Chern characters.

4. H. Gillet, C. Soulé, *K-théorie et nullité des multiplicités
   d'intersection*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris Sér. I Math. **300** (1985),
   71–74; and *Intersection theory using Adams operations*, Invent. Math.
   **90** (1987), 243–277. — Vanishing (M3) via Adams operations.

5. O. Gabber, proof of nonnegativity (M2), c. 1995, unpublished. Written
   accounts:
   - P. Berthelot, *Altérations de variétés algébriques (d'après
     A. J. de Jong)*, Séminaire Bourbaki 1995/96, Exposé 815, Astérisque 241
     (1997).
   - P. Roberts, *Recent developments on Serre's multiplicity conjectures:
     Gabber's proof of the nonnegativity conjecture*, L'Enseign. Math. **44**
     (1998), 305–324.

6. A. J. de Jong, *Smoothness, semi-stability and alterations*, Publ. Math.
   IHÉS **83** (1996), 51–93. — Alterations, the key input to Gabber's proof.

7. S. P. Dutta, M. Hochster, J. E. McLaughlin, *Modules of finite projective
   dimension with negative intersection multiplicities*, Invent. Math. **79**
   (1985), 253–291. — Failure of M2/M4 over non-regular rings.

## Books and surveys

8. P. Roberts, *Multiplicities and Chern Classes in Local Algebra*, Cambridge
   Tracts in Mathematics 133, Cambridge University Press, 1998.

9. W. Fulton, *Intersection Theory*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1998 (Ch. 18:
   localized Chern characters, Riemann–Roch).

10. [Wikipedia: Serre's multiplicity conjectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre%27s_multiplicity_conjectures)
    — overview and status.

## Partial results and current programs toward positivity (M4)

11. C. Skalit, *Positivity of intersection multiplicity over a two-dimensional
    base*, [arXiv:1510.05146](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.05146). — M4 for power
    series rings over complete 2-dimensional regular local bases.

12. S. P. Dutta, *On Serre intersection multiplicity conjecture*,
    [arXiv:1507.03915](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.03915). — Partial results
    under extra hypotheses.

13. A. Dubey (et al.), *Serre intersection multiplicity conjecture and Hodge
    theory*, [arXiv:1404.2622](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.2622). —
    Hodge-theoretic perspective in characteristic 0.

14. B. Bhatt, M. Hochster, L. Ma, *Lim Cohen-Macaulay sequences of modules*,
    [arXiv:2410.18372](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18372) (2024). — Existence of
    lim CM sequences in mixed characteristic would prove M4 in general;
    existence proved in characteristic $p$.

15. H. Dao et al., *Intersection multiplicity of Serre on regular schemes*,
    J. Algebra (2008),
    [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021869307005728).
